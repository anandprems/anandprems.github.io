Starting this blog

I'm starting this space to write short, regular notes — on ECG signal processing, deep learning architectures I'm reading about, and things that come up while teaching on ByteClassroom.

The plan is simple: shorter posts, more often, rather than long posts rarely. Some weeks that might be a quick summary of a paper. Other weeks it might be a note from class that a student asked a good question about.

If you're a student finding this — welcome. Feel free to reach out with questions any time.
