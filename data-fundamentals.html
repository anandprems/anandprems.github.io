Phase 1 Curriculum Pathway

Data Fundamentals — Understanding Data from Scratch

Data is the raw material behind every intelligent algorithm, medical diagnosis, and predictive system. This comprehensive 11-segment curriculum deconstructs data from the ground up: starting with why raw observations are not yet knowledge, categorizing quantitative measurements versus qualitative attributes, and mastering Stevens’ classic measurement scales (Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, and Ratio). We then explore real-world structural architectures—contrasting relational SQL tables with high-dimensional medical waveforms and flexible JSON/XML web APIs. Finally, we examine data provenance and acquisition methods, discovering how data origin dictates AI reliability and protects against model collapse.
